Insular Profitability Regimes in France 1903 - 1952

Jan Schulz, Omer Fetai, Angelo Secchi, Simone Alfarano & Mishael Milaković

Work in Progress

MECAPHIST
profitabilityfirmshistorical data

Summary

Using newly digitised historical balance-sheet data, this MECAPHIST project examines the distribution of firm profitability in France and asks whether relatively stable profitability regimes emerge across firms and over time.

Citation

Jan Schulz, Omer Fetai, Angelo Secchi, Simone Alfarano & Mishael Milaković (2026): Insular Profitability Regimes in France 1903 - 1952. Work in progress.
