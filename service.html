Editorial work, university service & memberships

I contribute to academic publishing, gender-sensitive research and several scholarly networks spanning economics, complexity science, political economy and economic history.

University service · since 2025

Center for Gender-Sensitive Research (ZGF)

I am a member of the University of Bamberg's Center for Gender-Sensitive Research and have served on its elected steering committee since 2026. My work there connects directly to research on gender inequality, intersectionality and privilege, including projects on perceived wage gaps and intersectional wage discrimination.

Associate Editor, SN Business & Economics

I serve as an Associate Editor of SN Business & Economics, a Springer Nature journal covering business, management, economics and finance. The role complements my reviewing and editorial activity across economics and the social sciences.
